# Social brand marks

These files preserve the original-color product marks used by the contact links.

- `gmail.svg` wraps the official 48 px Gmail PNG from
  `https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/1x/gmail_2020q4_48dp.png`
  without altering its image bytes. Source PNG SHA-256:
  `7e38235991985e298ee50270c73f30407ce4233977f0014e7c6edb53ec0cf1b3`.
- `huggingface.svg` is the official Hugging Face brand asset from
  `https://huggingface.co/datasets/huggingface/brand-assets/resolve/main/hf-logo.svg`.
  SHA-256: `942cad1ccda905ac5a659dfd2d78b344fccfb84a8a3ac3721e08f488205638a0`.

The site serves local copies so the icons do not add cross-origin requests or
depend on third-party availability at render time. The marks remain trademarks
of their respective owners and must not be recolored or modified.
